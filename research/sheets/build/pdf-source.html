2️⃣ Greg Isenberg's 5-Step Idea Discovery Method

ملحوظة مهمة: Gummy Search اتقفل، البديل الرسمي هو IdeaBrowser.

Step 1 — Find Trending Subreddits via IdeaBrowser

  • استخدم IdeaBrowser (بديل Gummy Search).
  • ركّز على communities من 10K-100K member — تجاوب على سؤال "Why now?".
  • راقب daily / weekly / monthly growth trends.
  • اختار niches فيها unfair advantage عندك.

Step 2 — Analyze Subreddit for Problems & Solutions

  • ضيف الـ subreddits لـ "audience" واحد في IdeaBrowser.
  • Pain & Anger — افحص الشكاوى الأكثر تكراراً.
  • Advice Requests — اللي الناس بتسأل عنه = blind spots في السوق.
  • Solution Requests — المنافسة الموجودة + الأدوات اللي الناس عايزة تكون موجودة.
  • 🔑 Key: دوّن الـ themes المتكررة، مش الـ one-offs.

Step 3 — Identify Creators in the Niche

  • استخدم Perplexity.ai تلاقي top influencers.
  • ابحث على YouTube عن أشهر فيديوهاتهم.
  • اقرأ الكومنتات — هي اللي هتفهمك احتياجات الجمهور.
  • طبّق على 20-25 فيديو عشان تاخد deep insights.
  • النتيجة: تفكر زي جمهورك المستهدف.

Step 4 — Wireframe Your Solution

  • على أساس الـ insights، اعمل sketch للمنتج/الخدمة.
  • استخدم Framer للـ prototyping السريع.
  • اختار اسم catchy + relevant + .com-able.
  • مثال Greg: DesignScientist.com — تصميم مركّز على conversion.

Step 5 — Create Content That Resonates

  • استخدم خاصية "Top Content" في IdeaBrowser.
  • حلّل اللي بيشتغل في الـ community (Memes? Long-form? Lists?).
  • اسأل AI عن content preferences.
  • 🤖 عامل Claude/ChatGPT كـ "Entrepreneurial Assistant".

7️⃣ مكتبة الـ Prompts (23)

كل Prompt جاهز للنسخ-اللصق في Claude أو ChatGPT. بدّل [النصوص بين الأقواس] بمحتواك.

🧠 Idea Discovery

Reddit Pain-Point Mining Claude / ChatGPT
You are an idea-discovery analyst. I'll paste 50 Reddit posts from [subreddit name]. For each post: 1) extract the explicit pain point, 2) categorize it (workflow / tool / cost / quality / experience / other), 3) score severity 1-5, 4) note if the user mentioned a willingness to pay. Output a Markdown table sorted by severity desc.
IdeaBrowser Three-Section Synthesis (Greg Step 2) Claude / ChatGPT
I'll paste 3 lists from IdeaBrowser for the audience [audience name]: (A) Pain & Anger top 30 posts, (B) Advice Requests top 30, (C) Solution Requests top 30. Tasks: 1) cluster recurring themes ACROSS all three lists; 2) for each cluster: severity 1-5 + frequency + 'why now' (timing trigger); 3) propose 5 brand/product ideas that solve the highest-scoring cluster; 4) flag the cluster with the LOWEST competition (most solution-requests, fewest existing tools).
Creator-Comments Insight Extractor (Greg Step 3) Claude / ChatGPT
I'll paste the top 30 comments from [N] popular YouTube videos by creators in [niche]. For each video: 1) what is the audience PRAISING (works); 2) what are they COMPLAINING about (gap); 3) what are they ASKING for that the creator hasn't covered (idea fuel). Then synthesize across all videos: 5 unmet needs the audience repeatedly expresses, ranked by frequency. End with 3 brand angles that would resonate.
Wireframe Spec (Greg Step 4) Claude
Idea: [paste 1-line idea]. Audience pain points: [paste top 5 from Greg Step 2]. Output a wireframe spec for a 1-page Framer prototype: 1) Hero (headline + subhead + CTA + visual concept); 2) Problem section (3 bullet pains in audience's exact language); 3) Solution section (how it works in 3 steps); 4) Social proof section (placeholder); 5) Pricing teaser; 6) Final CTA. Also propose 5 brand-name candidates that are catchy + relevant + .com-able.
Top-Content Style Analysis (Greg Step 5) Claude / ChatGPT
Here are the top 20 posts of all time in [subreddit/audience]: [paste]. Analyze: 1) format mix (memes / long-form / lists / screenshots / polls / videos); 2) tone (irreverent / earnest / educational / story); 3) hook patterns; 4) average length; 5) common visual styles. End with: 'For a brand entering this audience, the content style that will resonate is X because Y.' Be specific.
ChatGPT/Claude History Audit Claude
Below is a sample of my last 200 prompts to ChatGPT/Claude: [paste]. Group repeated themes. For each theme: 1) the recurring problem I face, 2) why no existing tool fully solves it for me, 3) a brand/product idea that would solve it, 4) who else likely shares this problem (audience size guess).
Friction Point Audit Claude
Here are entries from my friction log: [paste]. For each, identify: 1) the manual workflow, 2) the inefficiency, 3) a 1-line product idea that automates or removes it, 4) ease-to-build score 1-5.

🧠 Validation

4-Pillar Idea Scoring Claude
Score this idea: '[idea]'. For each pillar give a number 1-10 + 1-paragraph reasoning. Pillars: 1) Market Demand (search volume / Reddit complaints / 1-star competitor reviews); 2) Ease to Build (MVP in 30 days?); 3) Revenue Potential (price x audience size); 4) Personal Fit (interest + unfair advantage). End with: total /40, verdict, 3-step next action.
Onlyness Statement Claude
Using brand-context.md, draft 5 'onlyness' statements in the format: 'We're the only [category] that [unique mechanism] for [audience] who want [outcome].' Then critique each one for clarity, differentiation, and memorability, and recommend the top one with reasoning.

🧠 Competitor Analysis

Content Gap Analysis Claude + scraped competitor blogs/pages
You are a senior content strategist for [BrandName] (see brand-context.md). I scraped the following competitor blog/landing pages: [paste]. Tasks: 1) topic coverage map (Topic | Competitor | Depth 1-5); 2) 10 missing angles nobody covers well; 3) for each: working title / search intent / format / primary keyword / why it fits our brand voice; 4) rank by ROI (impact/effort).
Ad Intelligence Claude + Apify Meta Ad scrape
Here are competitor ads from Meta Ad Library [paste]: 1) extract headline / primary text / CTA / format for each; 2) group by messaging theme; 3) identify the 3 ads running longest = winning ads; 4) reverse-engineer the winning formula; 5) propose 5 ad angles for [BrandName].
Social Listening Claude + Reddit/X scrape
Search Reddit/X mentions of [Competitor] in last 30 days. 1) Extract top 20 high-engagement posts; 2) list top 10 user frustrations; 3) generate 5 content ideas that solve those frustrations from [BrandName]'s angle (in our brand voice).
Competitor Teardown Claude + Osum / SEMrush export
Here is competitor data: [paste]. Produce: 1) feature matrix (us vs them); 2) messaging hierarchy comparison; 3) pricing strategy analysis; 4) 5 weaknesses we can exploit; 5) 3 features they have that we MUST add; 6) 3 features they have that we should DELIBERATELY refuse to add (Zag).

🧠 Brand Strategy

Brand Strategist (Neumeier school) Claude with brand-context.md
Act as a senior brand strategist (Marty Neumeier school). Using brand-context.md, generate: 1) Positioning statement (1 sentence); 2) Onlyness statement; 3) 3 Zag opportunities ignored by competitors; 4) Brand archetype (Jung's 12) + reasoning; 5) Brand voice in 3 adjectives + 5 do's + 5 don'ts.
Brand Voice Skill Claude Skill
Skill: brand-voice. Always read brand-voice.md before writing. Apply: tone = [3 adjectives]. Always: [do's]. Never: [don'ts]. Before returning text, run a self-check: does it sound like a sentence we'd say? If not, rewrite. Output: final version + 1-sentence note on what you changed.

🧠 Local SEO

Local SEO Plan Claude
Act as an SEO expert specializing in local search. Our brand is [name] in [city]. Generate: 1) 30 long-tail keywords with local intent; 2) top 10 Google Business Profile categories; 3) 5 schema markup recommendations (LocalBusiness / FAQ / Product etc.); 4) outline for a 'Best [service] in [city]' pillar page; 5) 10 outreach targets for local backlinks.

🧠 Content

Content Calendar Claude with brand-context.md + content-pillars.md
Build a 30-day content calendar. Channels: Instagram / TikTok / LinkedIn / Blog. Format mix: 40% educational / 30% storytelling / 20% promo / 10% community. For each item: hook + body + CTA + hashtag set + posting time. Tied to brand pillars.

🧠 Data

CSV Profile + Insights Claude Code
Read [data.csv]. 1) Profile (shape / dtypes / null % / unique values). 2) Sanity checks; flag anomalies. 3) Hypothesis-driven analysis: top 3 drivers of [metric]; where do we lose users? 4) Output: markdown report + 5 charts (matplotlib).
Market Research Synthesis Claude (long context)
I've uploaded 10 PDF reports / podcast transcripts. Produce: 1) trends ranked 1-10; 2) emerging vocabulary; 3) quote bank (10 quotes with sources); 4) 5 strategic implications for our local brand.

3D Site Spec Claude Code
